
### Statistical Analysis: Toxic vs Non-toxic Comments

The Mann-Whitney U test was conducted to compare
comment lengths between toxic and non-toxic comments.

The results showed a statistically significant
difference between the two groups (p < 0.001).

The rank-biserial correlation was approximately
-0.237, indicating a modest difference in the
distributions, with toxic comments generally
tending to be shorter.

The median comment length was 22 words for toxic
comments and 38 words for non-toxic comments.

These findings suggest that comment length is
associated with toxicity labels. However, comment
length alone is insufficient to reliably identify
toxic content.
